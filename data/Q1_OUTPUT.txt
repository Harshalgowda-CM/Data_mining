Q1(c) - STAR SCHEMA / DIMENSIONAL MODEL
======================================================================

Star schema implemented with:

FACT TABLE:
    fact_sales

DIMENSION TABLES:
    dim_date
    dim_store
    dim_product
    dim_category

Dimension counts:

dim_date       : 366
dim_store      : 12
dim_category   : 14
dim_product    : 1,224

The fact table uses surrogate keys to avoid repeating store,
category and product attributes.

Product identity is resolved using:
    product_code + business date

The validity period is checked using:
    valid_from
    valid_to

This handles retired and reissued product codes correctly.


Analytical query used:

SELECT ds.store_id,
       dc.category_name,
       dd.month_name,
       dd.day_name,
       ROUND(SUM(fs.revenue),2) AS total_revenue
FROM fact_sales fs
JOIN dim_store ds
    ON fs.store_sk = ds.store_sk
JOIN dim_category dc
    ON fs.category_sk = dc.category_sk
JOIN dim_date dd
    ON fs.business_date = dd.full_date
GROUP BY ds.store_id,
         dc.category_name,
         dd.month,
         dd.month_name,
         dd.day_of_week,
         dd.day_name
ORDER BY ds.store_id,
         dd.month,
         dd.day_of_week,
         dc.category_name
LIMIT 30;
